Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Padel Tournament app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is developed and operated by an individual developer, not a company.

Who runs this app

Elisabeth Nygård Mesøy, an individual developer, publishes and maintains Padel Tournament. You can reach me at elisabethmesoy@gmail.com 

Information the app collects

The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or phone number. It collects only the following:

  • Anonymous device identifier. The first time you open the app, Firebase automatically assigns your device an anonymous identifier. This identifier lets your tournaments be saved and reloaded on the same device without requiring a login. It is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal account.
  • Tournament data you enter. This includes the names you type in for players, match scores, tournament format and settings. This data is stored in a cloud database (Firestore) and is associated only with your anonymous device identifier, not with your real identity.
  • Crash and diagnostic data. The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and basic diagnostic information (such as device model, operating system version, and app version) if the app crashes or encounters an error. This helps identify and fix bugs.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect your email address, phone number, or precise location.
  • It does not show advertising or share data with advertising networks.
  • It does not sell your data to any third party.
  • It does not access your contacts, photos, or other apps.

How this information is used

Player names and tournament data are used solely to run the app's core feature: letting you organize and save your padel tournaments. Crash and diagnostic data is used only to identify and fix technical issues.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Firebase (including Firestore and Crashlytics) as its cloud infrastructure provider. Firebase stores and processes the data described above on my behalf. Firebase's own privacy practices are described at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Data retention and deletion

Tournament data is retained for as long as it is associated with your device's anonymous identifier, or until you delete it within the app. If you would like all data associated with your device identifier permanently deleted from the database, contact elisabethmesoy@gmail.com with your request, and it will be removed.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no personal information such as name, email, or date of birth is required to use the app, age is not verified.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the app or applicable requirements. The date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact elisabethmesoy@gmail.com.
