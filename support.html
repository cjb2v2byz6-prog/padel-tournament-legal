Help with Padel Tournament

Padel Tournament helps you organize Americano, Mexicano, and Mixicano tournaments for your padel group — pairing players, assigning courts, and keeping score, without needing to sign up or create an account.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app saves your tournaments automatically on your device using an anonymous identifier, so there's no sign-up or login required.

Will I lose my tournaments if I reinstall the app or switch phones?

Because tournaments are tied to your device's anonymous identifier rather than an account, reinstalling the app or switching to a new device may not carry your tournament history over. Contact me if you run into this and I can help where possible.

How many players and courts do I need?

You need at least 4 players for one court. With 7 players, some will wait between rounds. From 8 players you'll need two courts, from 12 players three courts, and so on.

What's the difference between Americano, Mexicano, and Mixicano?

Americano keeps a fixed or rotating pairing schedule set in advance. Mexicano re-pairs teams after every round based on the live leaderboard. Mixicano does the same as Mexicano but always pairs one male with one female player.
